How to play

Ladders: move forward to climb up, backward to climb down. Release to hold; jump to leave. The touch joystick works too.

Move: W A S D. Jump: Space.
Look: mouse or arrow keys.
Hold left click to dig. Left click to hit.
Right click to place a block or eat.
Keys 1–9 pick an item. E opens your bag.
Point at a thing to see its name.
Press V to hear it again. Shift + V is slow.
Dig logs and stone. Then make tools.
Wolves come at night. Make a torch.

Touch screen: use the joystick to move, drag the view to look, and tap the action buttons. Tap a hotbar slot to select an item.
